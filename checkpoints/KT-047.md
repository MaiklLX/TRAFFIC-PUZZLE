# KT-047 — Roads as meaningful connections

Date: 2026-11-10

## Design goal
Move road building away from freehand asphalt drawing and toward a Mini Motorways-like connection puzzle: every road segment must serve a transport purpose by joining the existing network to another street, junction, or a real building/service entrance.

## Implemented in this step
- Road placement can begin only at an existing road, junction, or a recognized destination access point.
- Road placement must end at another recognized road/network or building access point.
- Isolated free-floating strokes are rejected.
- A valid new road must attach to the existing network at at least one end; isolated building-to-building fragments are rejected.
- Endpoints snap to the nearest useful connection point, reducing pixel-perfect touch requirements on phones.
- Building driveways are treated as valid entrances, with a short clearance allowance so a road can reach the building access without being rejected by the building's own collision radius.
- The road tool instructions now explain that the player is connecting useful points, not painting terrain.
- The initial village spine now stops short of the federal highway. The first mission therefore requires the player to create the missing connection; destination traffic becomes reachable only after the network is connected.

## Existing simulation retained
- Road graph and route finding.
- Direction-aware road edges.
- Vehicle movement and traffic spawning.
- Progressive road construction and undo.

## Not yet implemented
This is the first mechanical foundation, not a complete Mini Motorways feature set. Still required:
- A true starting scenario where only some buildings are connected and traffic demand grows over time.
- Clear colour-coded origin/destination demand pairs.
- A road-piece budget and meaningful upgrade choices.
- Traffic demand balancing, congestion feedback, and progression/missions.
- Robust road-network polygon union and polished junction geometry.
