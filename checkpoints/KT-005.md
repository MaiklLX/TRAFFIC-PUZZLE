# KT-005 — traffic-puzzle direction and road-network foundation

Date: 2026-11-10

## Product direction
TRAFFIC PUZZLE is a traffic-flow puzzle, not a city-building/economy simulator.
The settlement grows autonomously over the campaign. The player solves traffic problems
by connecting roads, routing vehicles, placing signs and signals, and managing congestion.

## Implemented in this checkpoint
- Replaced physically based standard materials with flatter Lambert shading.
- Disabled real-time shadow maps to reduce the heavy 3D appearance and rendering cost.
- Moved a house that intersected the village road and relocated roadside trees away from road lanes.
- Replaced the two independent back-and-forth toy-car loops with a shared route that starts on the federal highway, enters the village, and returns.
- Removed vehicle creation from the road-building action. Building a road never spawns a car.
- Removed the coin cost/reward loop from road construction and reframed the UI around traffic flow.
- Reworked road segments to use a flat asphalt surface with rounded junction fills.
- Added road-network attachment validation: at least one end of a new road must connect to an existing road.
- Added sampled collision checks against buildings, trees, and the pond. Invalid previews turn red and cannot be committed.

## Current limits — intentionally not claimed as complete
- Current live traffic is a small two-car demonstration on a predefined highway-to-village route.
- Dynamic arrivals, destinations, route choice, queues, traffic lights, and collision avoidance are not yet implemented.
- Settlement growth is not yet autonomous; current buildings remain the initial village layout.
- Road geometry is a first continuous-looking grid-path implementation, not a full spline-based road editor.
- The junction and signal tools still need their gameplay behavior.

## Next

junction-aware movement, queueing, and basic collision prevention. Then add autonomous settlement
milestones that introduce new traffic demand and new puzzle constraints without giving the player
